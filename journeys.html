Evergreen Homeschool

One family. One story.

A 10-year, National-Park journey through history — with every child at their own level.

Evergreen is a multi-age, family-style curriculum. Instead of a separate grade-level track for each child, the whole family travels the same trail through history — Bible, science, literature, art, poetry, writing, and nature all woven into each era — while the work meets each child right where they are. Five parks, two years each, four paths for four kinds of learner.

Rabbit Trails

Little paths worth wandering.

Some of the richest learning happens when you chase a rabbit — a question, a wonder, a thread that pulls you happily off the main trail. Rabbit trails are those short, delightful detours you take together. Here's one to begin with: a gentle weekly walk through who God is, one verse a day.

Bible Weekly

Pick a week. Each has an anchor verse to hide in your heart, plus a supporting verse for each day. Read them in your family's own Bible and translation.

More rabbit trails — a nature study, an artist study, a hymn a week — can join this warren over time.
