The Modern World

1900s – PresentTwo years · Explore + Deep TrailMulti-age · family-style

The modern age — world wars, wonders, and a world remade within living memory, right up to today.

What your family studies in Denali

Each park in Evergreen Homeschool is one era of history, studied for two years — first Explore, then Deep Trail — with Bible, science, literature, art, poetry, writing, geography, and nature study all woven into the same story. The whole family learns together while each child travels at their own path.

Year 1 · Explore Foundational stories

  1. A New Century — Flight, invention & change

    Cars, flight, and electricity — the turn into the fast-moving modern age.

    For you, teacher

    Grandparents are primary sources here — interview them. Living memory makes this era personal for every child at the table.

  2. A World at War — The World Wars

    A century of upheaval — the World Wars and the world they reshaped.

    For you, teacher

    Keep it human, not just strategic: one family’s story, one letter home. Shield the youngest from the worst while telling the truth to the oldest.

  3. A Changing World — Space, rights & rapid change

    The space age, the civil-rights movement, and a world changing by the day.

    For you, teacher

    Watch the moon landing, listen to a real speech. Primary footage lets your Wildflower and your Ranger feel the same goosebumps.

  4. Our World Today — The recent past & now

    The near past and the world your family actually lives in and shapes.

    For you, teacher

    Connect history to this week’s news, gently. Teaching them to think about today wisely is the whole point of the journey.

Year 2 · Deep Trail Deeper history & sources

  1. The Great Wars — Causes & consequences

    Primary accounts and the deeper why behind the century’s great conflicts.

    For you, teacher

    Move from “what happened” to “why, and what did it cost?” Let Rangers wrestle with hard questions while the family holds the human weight together.

  2. Modern Science — Atoms, DNA & the cosmos

    Modern physics, genetics, computers, and the reach into space.

    For you, teacher

    Hands-on still wins: a DNA-extraction from a strawberry delights every age. Let awe, not jargon, carry the science.

  3. Movements & Voices — The fight for justice, firsthand

    Civil rights and modern movements told through the people who lived them.

    For you, teacher

    Primary voices again — speeches, letters, memoirs. It grows conviction and compassion in a way no textbook summary can.

  4. Modern Literature — Voices of our time

    Twentieth-century classics and contemporary writers worth knowing.

    For you, teacher

    Choose books that spark real conversation. Preview for your family’s values, then talk openly — reading together is discipling together.

Four paths — every child at their own level

WildflowerYoung learnersWonder, stories, songs, and hands-on play — no writing required yet.
MeadowDeveloping learnersNarration (telling it back), notebooking, maps and timelines, and simple research.
RangerOlder learnersPrimary sources, analysis, independent research, and real writing.
HearthThe whole familyBible, poetry, art, nature, and read-alouds everyone shares together.
